before/after packet capture, so its resolver topology remains unproven.

## Decision and next step

Do not weaken block mode, allow arbitrary IPs, bump the action blindly, or
apply a shared resolver change. Successful samples outside the affected setup
prove the diagnostic works there; they do not establish fleet recovery. Reuse
this exact probe on recurrence in an affected pool and compare captured DNS
with the application/drop records before selecting one narrow remedy. Any remedy
still needs its own independent Sol review, external validation and a staged
consumer rollout. The separate invalid TestPyPI endpoint issue #1206 and other
CI admission/image-security holds are outside this experiment.
